Privacy Policy

Barem — Habit Tracker · Effective date: October 31, 2026

Barem is developed by Versec LLC ("we", "us"). We believe habit tracking should be private by design. This policy explains what data the app handles — and, mostly, what it does not handle.

1. Data we do not collect

Barem does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

2. Data stored on your device

All content you create in Barem — habits, check-ins, reminders, notes, and statistics — is stored only on your device in the app's private storage. It never leaves your phone. Deleting the app permanently removes this data from your device.

3. Apple Health (HealthKit) data

If you choose to enable it, Barem reads step counts and sleep data from Apple Health to display them on the Today screen. This data is:

  • Read-only — Barem never writes data to Apple Health.
  • Entirely on-device — it is processed locally and never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Optional — you grant access explicitly via the iOS permission dialog, and you can revoke it at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

4. Local notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

5. Children's privacy

Barem is a general-audience app and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app.
